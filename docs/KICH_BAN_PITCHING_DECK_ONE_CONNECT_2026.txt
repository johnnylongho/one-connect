[NOI DUNG SLIDE]:

- Dieu 11 (Explicit 2-Way Consent): Du lieu nhay cam tu dong che mo (Data Masking), chi hien thi khi duyet.

- Dieu 16 (Right to be Forgotten): Quyen yeu cau xoa vinh vien du lieu va vo hieu the NFC.
- Data Sovereignty: 3 che do tuy chon hien thi (Cong khai / Hoi vien / Rieng tu).

tu dong ghi nho boi canh gap go (Relationship Memory), va la nen tang dau tien tuan thu

qua Zalo, Camera hoac bat ky ung dung quet ma nao."

Cau 4: "Du an da san sang trien khai chua hay moi la y tuong?"
